# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (Initial Release)
- Initial architecture and Home Assistant Add-on specification.
- Multi-zone management and Cycle & Soak sequencing engine.
- Support for ESP32 microcontrollers over Wi-Fi, Ethernet (WT32-ETH01, Olimex PoE), and Bluetooth.
- Hardware fail-safe watchdogs to eliminate flood risk during network disconnects.
- Ingress dashboard support.
